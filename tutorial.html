Quick Start

Welcome, Trainer! Everything happens in Twitch chat while Scromf is live. Five steps and you are playing:

  1. 1
    A wild Pokémon appears on stream. Type !catch in chat. That is it, you are a Trainer now.
  2. 2
    Check your collection. Type !pokedex and the bot posts a link to your personal Pokédex page: every Pokémon you own, your stats and your bag.
  3. 3
    Collect 3 of the same Pokémon and type !evolve pikachu (or whichever you have 3 of). Watch it evolve live on stream.
  4. 4
    Earn Pokecoins just by watching and catching. Spend them at the Pokémart with !shop gb for a Great Ball and better odds.
  5. 5
    Pick a Buddy. Type !buddy equip eevee (any Pokémon you own) and it walks next to your name in chat.

New here? Say hi in chat and ask Scromf for your starter pack: Pokecoins and a few Great Balls to get going. Stuck at any point? Type !pokemon in chat for a quick reminder.

!catch Catching Pokémon

Wild Pokémon appear at random while Scromf streams. When one shows up on screen, throw a ball.

Type thisWhat happens
!catchThrows a free Poké Ball. Poké Balls never run out.
!catch gbThrows a Great Ball from your bag. Better odds. Also !catch great ball or !catch greatball.
!catch ubThrows an Ultra Ball. Even better odds. Also !catch ultra ball or !catch ultraball.
!catch mbThrows a Master Ball. Guaranteed catch. Also !catch master ball or !catch masterball.

Good to know

  • One throw per wild Pokémon. Pick your ball wisely.
  • Several Trainers can catch the same Pokémon. You are not stealing anyone's catch.
  • Results are announced when the encounter ends, so hang around for the reveal.
  • If you ask for a ball you do not have, a free Poké Ball is thrown instead.
  • Legendary and Mythic Pokémon are very hard to catch. When someone lands one, everyone in chat gets Pokecoins.
  • Get Great, Ultra and Master Balls from the Pokémart or with channel points.

Everything you catch is yours forever. !dex works as a shortcut for every command below.

Good to know

  • Your Pokédex website shows every Pokémon, your region progress, type mastery, catch stats, Trainer Journey, Buddy and Shop.
  • Use the filters on the website to find what is still missing, what is evolvable now, or who evolves randomly.
  • Mythic Pokémon like Mew and Celebi never appear in the wild. They come from special stream events.

!pokebag Your Pokébag

Balls, stones, berries and eggs live in your bag, together with your Pokecoins.

Type thisWhat happens
!pokebagLists every item you hold and your Pokecoins balance.

Good to know

  • Your bag has limits: 99 Great Balls, 99 Ultra Balls, 10 Master Balls, 3 Berries, 3 of each Stone, 1 Egg.
  • Your bag is also shown on the Shop tab of your Pokédex website.

!evolve Evolution

Collect copies of a Pokémon, then evolve them live on stream with sound and animation.

Type thisWhat happens
!evolve charmanderEvolves one Charmander into Charmeleon. Uses up 3 Charmander.
!evolve 7 meowthEvolves seven at once. Also !evolve meowth 7.
!evolve meowth allEvolves as many as you can. Also max.
!evolve eevee waterPicks a branch for Pokémon with several evolutions. Eevee: water, electric, fire, psychic, dark. Gloom: sun or leaf.
!evolveShort help in chat.

The rules

  • Copies. Most Pokémon need 3 copies per evolution. A few need more: Magikarp 20, Swablu 20, Feebas 10, Nincada 9.
  • Stones. Some need a stone from your bag as well. One stone per evolution, and it is used up.
  • Friendship. Some need a bond: raise that Pokémon's friendship to 2,500 with the Buddy system. Each evolution then spends 600 friendship.
  • Trade. Some only evolve through a trade with another Trainer.
  • Random. Wurmple becomes Silcoon or Cascoon, Clamperl becomes Huntail or Gorebyss, and you do not get to choose. Clamperl also needs a trade.
  • Nincada evolves into Ninjask, and a Shedinja appears too.
  • If a wild Pokémon is on screen, your evolution waits until the encounter finishes.

Stone evolutions

  • Thunder Pikachu, Eevee (Jolteon)
  • Moon Nidorina, Nidorino, Clefairy, Jigglypuff, Skitty
  • Fire Vulpix, Growlithe, Eevee (Flareon)
  • Leaf Gloom (Vileplume), Weepinbell, Nuzleaf
  • Sun Gloom (Bellossom), Sunkern
  • Water Poliwhirl (Poliwrath), Shellder, Staryu, Eevee (Vaporeon), Lombre

Friendship evolutions

  • Golbat, Chansey, Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Togepi
  • Eevee into Espeon (psychic) or Umbreon (dark)

Trade evolutions

  • Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter, Onix, Seadra, Scyther, Porygon
  • Poliwhirl into Politoed, Slowpoke into Slowking, Clamperl

!trade Trading

Swap Pokémon or items with another Trainer. Some Pokémon only evolve this way.

Type thisWhat happens
!trade @friend kadabra 3Offers 3 Kadabra to that Trainer. Leave the number off to offer one.
!trade machoke 3Your partner answers with what they put in. They have a minute to reply.
!trade @friend kadabra 3Type your offer again to confirm. The swap happens.

Good to know

  • One trade at a time in chat. Wait for the current one to finish.
  • Trade evolutions are automatic: if you both swap trade-evolvers and each of you ends up with 3 or more, both evolve on the spot.
  • Mew and Celebi cannot be traded.

!sell Releasing Pokémon for Pokecoins

Too many Raticate? Release the spares into the wild and get Pokecoins back.

Type thisWhat happens
!sell listEverything you can release right now, most valuable first.
!sell raticateReleases one Raticate and pays you.
!sell raticate 2Releases two. Also !sell 2 raticate.
!sell raticate allReleases every spare copy, always keeping one.

Good to know

  • Only fully evolved Pokémon can be released, and you always keep at least one copy.
  • Rarer Pokémon pay more, up to 10,000 Pokecoins for a Legendary. Pokémon evolved with a stone pay extra.
  • Eevee, Mew, Celebi, Jirachi, Deoxys and the Unown ! and ? can never be released.

!buddy Your Buddy

Equip one of your Pokémon as a Buddy. It walks next to your name in chat and your friendship grows while you watch together.

Type thisWhat happens
!buddyShows your current Buddy, or how to pick one.
!buddy equip eeveeMakes a Pokémon you own your Buddy. Friendship you earned with your old Buddy is kept.
!buddy greetSay hello. Banks the friendship you have earned so far and your Buddy greets you back.
!buddy pet+10 friendship. Uses a Pet Token. Tokens turn up in your bag while you watch.
!buddy feed+25 friendship. Uses a Berry from your bag.
!buddy golbatYour friendship points with any Pokémon line you name.

Good to know

  • Friendship is shared across a whole evolution line: points earned with Zubat count for Golbat too.
  • Friendship unlocks evolutions for Golbat, Chansey, Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Togepi and Eevee's Espeon and Umbreon.
  • Berries come from the Pokémart or channel points.

!egg Eggs

Eggs hatch into baby Pokémon you cannot catch in the wild.

Type thisWhat happens
!eggShows how long is left. When the Egg is ready, this hatches it live on stream.

Good to know

  • An Egg hatches after 20 hours of watching. Time only counts while you are in the stream.
  • One Egg at a time: hatch it before you pick up another.
  • What can hatch: Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Togepi, Tyrogue, Smoochum, Elekid, Magby, Azurill, Wynaut.
  • Get Eggs from the Pokémart or with channel points. Your Egg starts incubating the moment you get it, nothing to type.

!shop The Pokémart

Spend your Pokecoins on balls, stones, berries and eggs. Everything you buy goes straight into your Pokébag, and all sales are final: nothing can be sold back. !buy works exactly like !shop, and so do !pokemart, !pokeshop and !mart.

Type thisWhat happens
!shopThe price list: every item on the shelf and what it costs.
!shop gbBuys one Great Ball. Item name right after !shop, nothing else needed. gb, great ball and greatball all work.
!shop 10 gbBuys ten. Also !shop gb 10.
!shop ubUltra Ball. Also !shop mb for a Master Ball, !shop berry, !shop fire for a Fire Stone, !shop egg.

Good to know

  • Short names work everywhere: gb, ub, mb, berry, fire, water, thunder (or electric), leaf (or grass), moon, sun, egg. Full names and plurals work too.
  • !shop stone asks which stone you mean.
  • You never pay for something you cannot carry: if your bag is full, nothing is charged.
  • Buying a Master Ball is a big moment. The whole stream will know.
  • Poké Balls are free and are not in the shop. God Balls cannot be bought at all.

!pokecoins Pokecoins

The currency of the stream. !coins works too.

Type thisWhat happens
!pokecoinsYour current balance.
!pokecoins helpAll the ways to earn more.
!win coinsHow many Pokecoins you have won from Scromf's giveaways and challenges.

How to earn Pokecoins

  • Watch. Pokecoins trickle in while you are in the stream. Subscribers earn a little extra.
  • Catch. Every catch pays. Catching a Legendary pays a lot, and everyone in chat gets a cut.
  • Stay through ad breaks. Everyone in chat gets 150 Pokecoins after every ad break, and a !bonus opens for 90 seconds on top.
  • Release spares with !sell.
  • Win. Scromf hands out Pokecoins for predictions, challenges and good answers to the question of the day.
